## Interpretation

This experimental dataset presents proposed directed connections among Governance and Risk functions. Every relationship is classified as Hypothesis. Inclusion does not establish empirical validation, causation, effectiveness, or assurance. Direction preserves the research proposal; it does not demonstrate an observed flow. The neutral label proposed connection deliberately omits the underlying relationship taxonomy. This is a partial topology, not a complete model. Absent connections do not establish the absence of a relationship. Node placement, centrality, and connectivity must not be treated as evidence of importance or correctness. Public feedback may inform later review; it does not automatically change canonical confidence classifications.
