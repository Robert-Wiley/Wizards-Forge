# Contributing to Governance Physics

Contributions do not need to agree with the current model.

Useful contributions include:
- counterexamples,
- alternate taxonomies,
- new datasets,
- domain applications,
- failed reproductions,
- revised relationship semantics,
- mathematical critiques,
- visualization improvements,
- experimental designs,
- and documentation that makes the work easier to understand or test.

## Suggested contribution pattern

Describe:
- **Claim or object:** What are you examining?
- **Context:** Where did you test it?
- **Method:** What did you do?
- **Result:** What happened?
- **Evidence:** What supports the result?
- **Interpretation:** What might it mean?
- **Proposed state change:** Should the current research state change?

## Evidence states

Do not present a hypothesis as a validated finding.

A contribution can be valuable while remaining uncertain.

## Forks

Independent forks are encouraged. If your work intentionally diverges from the current model, document the divergence rather than forcing compatibility.

## Respect the safety boundary

Do not submit credentials, client evidence, personal data, restricted information, employer-owned material, or proprietary third-party content without appropriate rights and authorization.
