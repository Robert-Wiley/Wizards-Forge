# Research History

This document is a navigational history, not a polished origin story.

The goal is to preserve the visible path by which the research changed.

## Development arc

### Convergent Governance
Early work focused on integrating governance frameworks, assurance, evidence, processes, and controls into a more unified operating model.

### Governance Intelligence
The research expanded toward the question: **How do we know an assessment has meaningful assurance?**

This shifted emphasis from framework mapping toward evidence quality, signal reliability, state, and decision confidence.

### Seams and state propagation
Attention moved to the boundaries between governance objects and systems: how signals are extracted, constructed, translated, and propagated across seams.

### Quantum-Inspired Governance Dynamics
The research began treating drift, instability, lifecycle state, and changing governance conditions as first-class analytical concerns.

### Theory of Governance Dynamics
Previously separate ideas began consolidating into a broader theory of governance state, telemetry, relationships, assurance, and change.

### Governance Physics
The emerging research program began treating governance, risk, compliance, assurance, evidence, and organizational behavior as an interacting system suitable for structural, computational, and empirical study.

### GRC topology
A baseline corpus of 286 Governance, Risk, and Compliance objects was created, followed by a governed relationship registry.

### Relational research environment
Notion became the canonical Atlas, Obsidian the relational discovery layer, GitHub the versioned bridge, GitLab the protected validation environment, and the live topology the communication layer.

### Public interactive topology
The first public-review map exposed a highly concentrated Compliance component with Governance and Risk forming an isolated outer halo.

This was not hidden or cosmetically corrected. It revealed that the public-review edge layer was incomplete: the released edges were Compliance-to-Compliance relationships. The visualization therefore became evidence about the state of the research itself.

## Principle

**Do not rewrite history to make the research look inevitable.**

Future entries should record both successful and failed directions.
