# Governance Physics — Open Research Commons

> **This work is intentionally unfinished.**

Governance Physics is an evolving research environment for studying governance, risk, compliance, assurance, organizational behavior, evidence, and change as interacting systems rather than isolated disciplines.

The purpose of this Commons is not to present a finished theory. It is to expose the work to examination.

**Test it. Challenge it. Reproduce it. Break it. Extend it.**

## Current research surface

The current GRC topology contains a canonical research corpus of **286 GRC objects** and **715 governed relationships** in the private research environment. A governed public-review layer is being released progressively.

The interactive topology is therefore a **living snapshot**, not a claim of completion.

## Core principle

**Preserve the path, not just the destination.**

Development history matters. Failed hypotheses, broken models, revised assumptions, contradictory evidence, and abandoned branches should remain visible when they are useful for understanding how the research evolved.

## Research loop

**Publish → Observe → Challenge → Break → Repair → Validate → Republish**

## Canonicality

The public Commons is a communication and collaboration layer. Canonical research records remain governed in the GRC Atlas. Public artifacts should identify their version and evidence state.

## Safety boundary

Open research does not mean unrestricted disclosure. Client data, employer-owned information, credentials, restricted material, personal data, contractual information, and proprietary third-party content are excluded.
