# Governance Physics Open Research Charter — v0.1

## 1. Purpose

Governance Physics is maintained as an open, evolving research program intended to be tested, challenged, reproduced, extended, contradicted, and improved.

The objective is not preservation of ownership.

**The objective is continuity of inquiry.**

## 2. Nothing is sacred

No theory, relationship, taxonomy, formula, topology, assumption, interpretation, or architectural choice is exempt from examination.

A useful challenge is more valuable than agreement.

## 3. Preserve the history

Research evolution should remain inspectable.

For meaningful revisions, preserve:
- what was believed,
- what evidence existed,
- what failed,
- what contradicted the model,
- what changed,
- what survived,
- and what remains unresolved.

Broken states are research evidence when they explain later decisions.

## 4. Research states are explicit

Ideas should be distinguishable as hypotheses, supported findings, confirmed relationships, validated findings, deprecated constructs, or unresolved questions.

Publication does not imply certainty.

## 5. Reproduction over persuasion

Where possible, publish sufficient structure, methods, assumptions, datasets, schemas, and version information for another person to reproduce or challenge a result.

## 6. Forks are welcome

Researchers, practitioners, students, engineers, and organizations are encouraged to adapt the work to environments not anticipated by the original research.

A fork that produces a different result is not a failure of participation. It is new evidence.

## 7. Disagreement is productive

Counterexamples, failed reproductions, contradictory datasets, alternate taxonomies, mathematical objections, and domain-specific failures should be treated as inputs to the research program.

## 8. Protect people, not ideas

Open research must not expose client evidence, employer intellectual property, credentials, personal data, contractual restrictions, proprietary third-party material, or restricted information.

## 9. Canonicality is governance, not authority over thought

Canonical records exist so the current research state can be located and reproduced. They do not prevent independent interpretations, forks, or competing models.

## 10. Legacy

The strongest outcome is not that this work remains unchanged.

The strongest outcome is that future researchers can understand where it came from, improve it, and take it somewhere the original researchers did not imagine.
