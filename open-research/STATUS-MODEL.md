# Research State Model

The Commons distinguishes publication from certainty.

Suggested states:

- **Hypothesis** — plausible relationship or claim requiring validation.
- **Supported** — multiple observations or sources provide meaningful support.
- **Confirmed** — the relationship or claim is strongly established within the current research context.
- **Validated** — independently tested against an explicit method or validation criterion.
- **Contested** — meaningful contradictory evidence exists.
- **Deprecated** — retained for history but no longer used in the current model.
- **Unresolved** — evidence is insufficient or conflicting.

These states may evolve as the research matures.

The objective is not to force certainty.

The objective is to make uncertainty visible.
