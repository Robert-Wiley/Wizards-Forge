# Wizard’s Forge

Wizard’s Forge is a governed multi-agent research and automation environment designed to develop, test, and orchestrate specialized AI agents under explicit authority boundaries, evidence requirements, human approval gates, and shared governance controls.

GRS-001 — Governance Research Scout  
Purpose: discover, evaluate, and structure research relevant to governance theory and Governance Intelligence.
